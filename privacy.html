Privacy Policy – Chronicle Auto-Poster

Chronicle Auto-Poster is a private publishing tool used to upload videos to the TikTok account of the authenticated user.

Data we collect

  • This tool is designed as an internal utility and does not collect personal data from the general public.
  • It may store technical information such as video file paths, captions, and render logs on the owner’s own systems for the purpose of preparing and publishing videos.

Use of TikTok API

  • Chronicle Auto-Poster uses the TikTok API only to upload videos and manage posts on the authenticated user’s TikTok account.
  • It does not access, sell, or share user data with third parties.

Third-party services

Videos may be hosted or processed by other services (e.g., storage providers or rendering tools) as part of the publishing workflow. Any such services are used only to prepare and publish videos.

Data sharing and selling

  • Chronicle Auto-Poster does not sell personal information.
  • Data is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Your control

  • You can revoke the app’s access via your TikTok account settings at any time.
  • Removing access will prevent the app from uploading or managing content on your account.
